<h2>Project Overview</h2>

Leads Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze lead performance, qualification, conversion, profitability, and team activity.

The dashboard provides a structured analytical view of lead volume, lead status, Account Manager performance, Customer Success activity, Lead Generator performance, lead profitability, and Hardware vs Software distribution through interactive visualizations and filters.

<h2>Business Objectives</h2>

- Analyze overall lead performance and sales pipeline activity.
- Track qualified, not qualified, won, and lost leads.
- Analyze lead performance across Account Managers and Team Leaders.
- Identify lead conversion and win-rate patterns.
- Analyze lead profitability and business opportunities.
- Understand Hardware vs Software lead distribution.
- Identify the main reasons behind lost leads.
- Analyze leads by customer roles and job titles.
